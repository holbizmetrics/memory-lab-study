0.90.28

Where the synthesis architecture stands

4 of 4 LFO sources

Four independent sources, each with four shapes and bipolar/unipolar choices, note reset/free run and tempo sync. All four sources have listening approval. Extra hardware shape/range coverage remains.

4 independent envelopes

Four dAHDSR contours with expanded pitch/filter/PWM destinations. Linear contours currently approximate the hardware envelope character; exponential behavior and timing are next gaps.

8 voices, 4 editable parts

Layers, split, channels and complete recall are approved. Fixed reservations remain our policy; hardware allocation details need evidence.

Oscillator foundations

Saw and pulse sources, tuning, PWM and noise exist. These are not yet two fully equipped hardware VCO models.

Classic and modern sweeps

Both profiles and modern bandwidth are implemented and approved. Exact hardware laws and profile/resonance modulation remain separate work.

Playable workflow

Patch files, INIT, Dice, drive bypass and expression controls exist. Presets help playing; they do not close a major synthesis gap.

There is no completion percentage here: milestones differ greatly in size. Feature coverage does not establish a faithful Relic emulation. Hardware sound/control matching requires reference comparisons. This page is a project snapshot, updated with the source—not a live feed.
