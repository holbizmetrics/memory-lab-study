# Memory Lab Study v0.13.0-alpha.1

SOUNDS opens a category/starting-sound browser with eight original patches:
Clean Foundation, Round Bass, Soft Fifth, Slow Air, Glass Pluck, Noise Tap,
Breath Swell and Clockwork Pulse. Browsing does not change sound. LOAD SOUND
applies a complete factory-based patch, preserves current output and bypasses
both drives. UNDO LOAD restores the prior complete sound until another sound
edit invalidates it. SAVE/LOAD remain the native patch-file actions. Selection
and one-step history are session conveniences, not saved preset metadata.

Also includes a local offline route map at roadmap/index.html: 12 clickable
0.9-0.20 stops, a draggable/keyboard route cursor, details and explicit synthesis
coverage limits. MILESTONES.md distinguishes workflow from architecture and
technical validation from Holger's musical approval. Future scopes are proposals,
not guaranteed completion by 0.20 or a claim of faithful hardware emulation.

Compatibility: no DSP changes; unchanged 49 host parameter IDs/order, schema 8,
host state and legacy migration. Presets validate through the existing full-patch
loader; output at zero remains muted. Factory INIT retains its original sound
and shared-drive default. New Clean Foundation is distinct from INIT.

Validation: Windows VST3/standalone built; all 10 CTest checks passed (50.11 s).
The preset test covers full-patch validation, independence from previous edits,
output/mute preservation, both drives zero, file-encoding/host roundtrips,
invalid indices, Undo/edit invalidation and passive category selection. All eight
presets rendered with eight simultaneous notes at 44.1/48/96 kHz (24 renders;
largest peak 0.776298 at output 0.25). Actual VST3 scan/load/MIDI/arp/state/release/
reload checks passed; eight actual-module E-minor listening captures are finite,
audible and below full scale (largest peak 0.366332). Default and migrated schema-7
clocked audio are byte-identical to verified 0.12 captures. Normal and compact
SOUNDS editor snapshots inspected. 208 page switches preserve sound parameters.
Route-map script parses and its 12 selections/cursor/navigation passed a DOM
fixture check; actual browser layout/interaction has not been verified.

Listening: Holger confirmed 0.12's three expression controls and Dice drive
bypass/Undo on 2026-11-06. He explicitly chose to test 0.13 starting sounds with
0.14's second LFO, then stop for that musical review before four-LFO expansion.
0.13 starting-sound musical approval is pending. No new 0.13 plugin installation
is claimed here; 0.12 stays installed until the combined update is verified.

Limits: finite original starting bank, not manufacturer patches or a user-file
library manager. Loads replace edits; output preservation is not loudness
normalisation. Play fresh notes after loading due to existing envelope latching.
Host automation may override patches. Full FL recording/undo/project-reload and
live patch-folder persistence checks remain open. Source-only prerelease, no
binary assets pending distribution licensing.
