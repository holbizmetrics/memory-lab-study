Exact frozen listening reference

The live voice uses two oscillators, a low-pass filter and velocity-sensitive envelopes. It approximates the source recipe; it is not sample-identical. No automatic RMS matching is applied to live playing. Depth fades smoothly below drive 2; the frozen takes are unchanged. This exploratory worklet is not a production VST3, and its resampling does not guarantee alias-free output.
